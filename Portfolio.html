About Me

Hi! My name is Thomas Smith. I'm a Game Developer here in Brisbane, Australia that specialises in Unity. I'm studying at SAE University College for a Batchelor Of Game Development (Programming).

I have experience developing for desktop, mobile, XR devices, and web environments using Unity and C#. My Focus has been on Networking and XR/VR Systems with my Capstone Project being a 2-24 player VR title which I developed the systems for (wayyyyy too many people to try and playtest at once for VR oof).

Throughout my studies and career, I've had the opportunity to work on diverse projects both as a team member and as a project leader. I'm leading production of Sanctuary Protocol, a funded VR project here in Brisbane, where I'm managing the development pipeline and working with our small team to release on Meta and Steam.
